# Governance

Raid Signal is currently maintained by `@QTtrash`, who has final responsibility
for roadmap, releases, security response, licensing, and repository policy.

Changes are discussed through issues and reviewed through pull requests. The
maintainer may delegate ownership through `CODEOWNERS` as the contributor base
grows. Decisions prioritize user safety, protocol compatibility, privacy,
maintainability, and the documented licensing boundary.

The project does not promise acceptance of every contribution or a fixed response
time. Conduct applies equally to maintainers and contributors.
