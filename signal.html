PRIVACY MODEL

The relay carries ciphertext, not your raid.

Raid Signal sends only encrypted map ID, coordinates, heading, callsign and timestamp. It never sends screenshots, Tarkov logs, account information, quests, custom pins or historical positions.

  • The VPS and hosting provider can see connection IP addresses, timing and room activity.
  • Anyone with an invitation can join. Keep it private and create a new room if it leaks.
  • A modified client with the key could forge a position; official phone clients are view-only.
