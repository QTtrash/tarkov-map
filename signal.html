PRIVACY / THREAT MODEL

The relay carries ciphertext. You carry the loot.

Raid Signal sends only encrypted map ID, coordinates, heading, callsign, sequence, and timestamp.

  • No screenshots or Tarkov logs
  • No account information
  • No quests or custom pins
  • No historical positions

The VPS and hosting provider can still see IP addresses, timing, and room activity. Anyone possessing an invitation can join, and a modified client with the key could forge a position. Keep invitations private.
